# Vision & Objectives - 2025-10-23

## Mission
Maximize shareholder wealth through AI-driven trading systems.

## Strategic Objectives (Q4 2025)

### Primary
1. **Profitable Trading System Operational** (Target: Paper trading validated strategy by Dec 2025)
   - Metric: Sharpe ratio > 1.5 in backtest, positive returns in paper trading
   - Success: Shareholder approves capital deployment

### Supporting
2. **Risk-First Architecture** (Target: Risk system validated in backtesting by Nov 2025)
   - Metric: Zero backtest violations of position/drawdown limits
   - Priority: Risk > Profitability > Efficiency

3. **Autonomous AI Operations** (Target: 90% of development via orchestrator by Dec 2025)
   - Metric: % tasks completed without human code intervention
   - Success: CEO + orchestrator + specialized agents self-sustaining

## Constraints
- **Free-only**: All technologies, data sources, services must be free (no paid APIs/subscriptions)
- **Human approval required**: Trading strategies, risk limits, capital deployment, regulatory compliance
- **AI-optimized**: No human-aesthetic documentation (CLAUDE.md authoritative), token efficiency prioritized

## Success Definition
System generates consistent risk-adjusted returns (Sharpe > 1.5) that shareholder authorizes for capital deployment.
