# SolaraAlpha Vision & Mission

## Mission
Maximize shareholder wealth through AI-driven, risk-managed trading systems that operate with institutional-grade reliability.

## Vision
Become the world's first fully autonomous, AI-native proprietary trading firm generating consistent, uncorrelated returns across multiple strategies, asset classes, and timeframes.

## Core Principles

### Priority Hierarchy
1. **Risk Management** - Preserve capital and maintain operational reliability
2. **Profitability** - Generate consistent, risk-adjusted returns
3. **Operational Efficiency** - Scale through automation and standardization

### Strategic Objectives (2025)

**Q1 2025: Foundation**
- Establish core infrastructure (data, risk, execution)
- Deploy first relative value strategy (pairs trading)
- Achieve >85% test coverage on all systems
- Implement real-time risk monitoring

**Q2 2025: Diversification**
- Deploy 3+ uncorrelated strategies across 2+ asset classes
- Implement centralized execution optimization
- Establish automated capital allocation (PAC)
- Achieve positive Sharpe ratio >1.0

**Q3 2025: Scale**
- Expand to 5+ strategies across 3+ asset classes
- Implement factor-based portfolio construction
- Deploy advanced risk analytics (VaR, CVaR, stress testing)
- Achieve 15%+ annual return target

**Q4 2025: Maturity**
- Operate 8+ uncorrelated strategies
- Multi-timeframe coverage (HF to long-term)
- Full governance layer operational (IRM, PAC, RTOC)
- Institutional-grade monitoring and reporting

## Competitive Advantage

1. **AI-Native Architecture**: No legacy systems, pure AI-to-AI workflows
2. **Hybrid Structure**: Centralized risk/infra + decentralized strategy development
3. **Free Technology Stack**: Zero infrastructure costs through open-source tools
4. **Rapid Innovation**: Sub-agents enable parallel strategy development
5. **Institutional Discipline**: HRO mindset, preoccupation with failure

## Constraints & Trade-offs

**Constraints:**
- All technologies, data sources, services must be free
- AI-only operations (no human traders)
- Single-process orchestration design
- GitHub-based state management

**Accepted Trade-offs:**
- Latency for reliability (handoff protocols include friction)
- Simplicity over cutting-edge complexity
- Correctness over speed
- Documentation overhead for async transparency

## Strategic Risks & Mitigations

**Risk 1: Market Regime Change**
- Mitigation: Multi-strategy diversification, continuous monitoring
- Trigger: Strategy Sharpe <0.5 for 30 days → reduce allocation

**Risk 2: Technology Failure**
- Mitigation: >85% test coverage, monitoring, circuit breakers
- Trigger: System downtime >1 hour → human escalation

**Risk 3: Data Quality Issues**
- Mitigation: Multiple free data sources, validation pipelines
- Trigger: Data gaps >5 minutes → halt affected strategies

**Risk 4: Over-optimization (Curve Fitting)**
- Mitigation: Walk-forward testing, out-of-sample validation
- Trigger: Live performance <80% of backtest → review strategy

**Risk 5: Correlation Breakdown**
- Mitigation: Real-time correlation monitoring, factor analysis
- Trigger: Strategy correlation >0.6 → PAC reallocation
